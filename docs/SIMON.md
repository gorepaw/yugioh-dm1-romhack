# Simon Muran — deck, drops, rewards (proposal, kaizo)

Duelist 13 / pool 16 · **Stage 3, the lone gate before Pegasus.** Nothing here is in the
ROM yet.

Identity: an ancient Egyptian guardian-mage. His stock deck is 19 Spellcasters (weak,
800–1700) — kept as the beatable body — with four **Egyptian guardians** layered on as
the threat. Additive, like Yami (not full-replace like Pegasus).

  the 4000 Gods are higher).
- **Exxod (0/4000)** is a pure wall — 0 ATK, but its 4000 DEF matches the Gods and is
  the highest DEF of any non-God. A real "you shall not pass" card for a gatekeeper.

### Fusion — Theinen mirrors the real summon

*Andro Sphinx + Sphinx Teleia → Theinen the Great Sphinx.* In the TCG, Theinen is
special-summoned by tributing both sphinxes; that maps cleanly to our 2-material fusion.
All three sit in the **fusion zone (#1–#300)**; Exxod is standalone (free-tail eligible).

drops with Theinen **suppressed** (it's a fusion): Teleia/Andro uncommon, Exxod rare,
Theinen rarest. Tunable — or say "every card except Gods" like Pegasus if you'd rather.
