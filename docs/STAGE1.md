# Stage 1 — the friends (proposal, kaizo)

The four **easy tutorial opponents** (Yugi 9, Tristan 10, Joey 11, Bakura 12). They're
"set and pass" in the stock game, so additions are light and their strong cards stay
**rare** in-deck — the friends must remain beatable first opponents. Signatures become
their top rewards. All names/flavour verified ≤18 tiles.

## Yugi (pre-Yami) — the Knights (+3)

Distinct from Yami (who has the Dark Magician / God package): Stage-1 Yugi is his
**Royal Knights**, a clean Warrior trio.

| card | ATK/DEF | type | flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Queen's Knight | 1500/1600 | Warrior | `A knight sworn` / `to the crown.` |
| King's Knight | 1600/1400 | Warrior | `Loyal blade of` / `the sovereign.` |
| Jack's Knight | 1900/1000 | Warrior | `A gallant knight` / `of the realm.` |

> ⚠ Queen's Knight (1500/1600 Warrior) is a stat-twin of Mai's new Amazon Swords Woman.
> Different decks, but mechanically identical — nudge one if you want them distinct.
